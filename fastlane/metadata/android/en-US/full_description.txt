TickTock is an open-source Android app that helps set the beat of a pendulum clock.

KEY FEATURES:

- Real-time beat tracking from microphone audio
- Period measurement in milliseconds
- Asymmetry measurement in milliseconds and percent
- Gauge visualization from -25% to +25%
- Color-coded asymmetry indicator for quick feedback
- Start/Stop acquisition with live status text

USAGE:

1. Set the initial guess period (default 1200 ms)
2. Tap Start and grant microphone permission
3. Wait while TickTock locks to the beat
4. Read Period, Asymmetry [ms], and Asymmetry [%]

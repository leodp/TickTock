<p>TickTock is an open-source Android app that helps set the beat of a pendulum clock.</p>

<p><strong>KEY FEATURES:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Real-time beat tracking from microphone audio</li>
<li>Period measurement in milliseconds</li>
<li>Asymmetry measurement in milliseconds and percent</li>
<li>Gauge visualization from -25% to +25%</li>
<li>Color-coded asymmetry indicator for quick feedback</li>
<li>Start/Stop acquisition with live status text</li>
<li>Live <code>G:</code> indication of the automatic gain-control multiplier; the value freezes after fit lock</li>
<li>Beat-log export with 0.1 ms precision</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>USAGE:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Set the initial guess period (default 1200 ms)</li>
<li>Tap Start and grant microphone permission</li>
<li>Wait while TickTock locks to the beat</li>
<li>Read Period, Asymmetry [ms], and Asymmetry [%]</li>
</ol>
